about the creator

Hi! My name is Leisha. I’m a software engineer, cat lover, and most importantly, a HUGE matcha lover. I grew up on a small island in Hawaiʻi, where my first introduction to matcha was through a basketball teammate in middle school. From crappy matcha packets at home to Starbucks runs whenever I went off-island, my love for matcha slowly grew. Moving to San Francisco for college changed EVERYTHING. My first ceremonial-grade matcha at Kiss of Matcha opened up a whole new world, and I’ve been obsessed ever since. Now, I have my own little matcha station at home, love anything and everything matcha, and am constantly on the hunt for the next matcha that’ll blow my mind. I’m no expert, just a matcha lover sharing my journey one cup at a time!
