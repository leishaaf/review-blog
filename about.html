about the creator

Hello! My name is Leisha. I’m a software engineer, cat lover, and more importantly (for this blog), a HUGE matcha lover. I grew up on a small island in Hawaiʻi, where there wasn’t access to trendy mainstream foods. My first introduction to matcha was through a basketball teammate in middle school while traveling off-island. I still remember the first time the earthy and weird taste of a Starbucks matcha became a part of my world. I remember liking it and surprising myself that I did. From there, my journey with Matcha grew. In high school, my local restaurant started serving matcha lattes that were hit or miss. Nonetheless, I still ordered every time. Then, I moved to San Francisco for college, which changed everything. My first ceremonial-grade matcha at Kiss of Matcha opened up a whole new world, and I’ve been obsessed ever since. Now, I have my own little matcha station at home, love anything and everything matcha, and am constantly on the hunt for the next matcha that’ll blow my mind. I’m no expert, just a matcha lover sharing my journey one cup at a time!¸
